PROCESS

How the Image Evolves

01

Generate

A population of genomes is created from translucent triangles, circles and detail dots.

02

Render

Every genome is rendered in layer order onto an off-screen canvas to produce a candidate image.

03

Compare

Candidate pixels are compared with the target. Progressive resolution allows early generations to be evaluated much more quickly.

04

Focus

Error-guided mutations can concentrate new changes in areas where the evolved image differs most strongly from the target.

05

Select

The strongest genomes survive while mutated descendants compete to replace weaker candidates.

06

Refine

Mutation strength falls and fitness resolution rises as evolution moves from broad composition towards increasingly fine detail.

GENOME

The Artwork Is Data

Every evolved image can be represented as an ordered collection of geometric shapes. Position, size, colour, transparency and layer order can therefore be stored independently of the rendered PNG.
